# Demo headshots: sources

Every photo here is an AI-generated face of a person who does not exist, taken from the
[Synthetic Faces High Quality (SFHQ) dataset](https://github.com/SelfishGene/SFHQ-dataset), part 3 (StyleGAN2),
© 2022 David Beniaguev, MIT License (see LICENSE-SFHQ.txt). Resized to 128×128; metadata removed.
They were downloaded from the 256px mirror at https://huggingface.co/datasets/pravsels/SFHQ_256 (shard 1),
which keeps SFHQ's original file names. The names attached to them in the demo are fictional.
